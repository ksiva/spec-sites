Made by people who still love making things.

Vesper Row is an independent production company. We produce commercials and branded films for agencies and brands across Canada and the US.

We keep crews small and decisions close to the camera. Every job has one producer from the first call to the final grade, so nothing gets lost between people.

Our directors choose their projects. That is why the work looks like theirs.
