I started as a camera assistant and never stopped looking through the lens.

I direct commercials and brand films from Toronto. Most of my work is fragrance, travel, sport and food: subjects where light and texture carry the story.

I work with small crews and prefer practical effects. If something can be done for real in front of the camera, I would rather shoot it than fix it later.
